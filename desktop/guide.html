Faithful Keys on your desktop

Choose Guided Mode for a learning path or Explore Mode to practice freely. Your progress and settings are saved on this computer.

Connect your keyboard

  1. Connect your USB MIDI keyboard and install its manufacturer's driver if required.
  2. For Bluetooth MIDI, pair and connect the keyboard using your operating system or the manufacturer's MIDI software. On Mac, use Audio MIDI Setup → Window → Show MIDI Studio → Bluetooth configuration. Bluetooth audio pairing alone does not create a MIDI input.
  3. Open MIDI Setup in Faithful Keys, choose Connect MIDI keyboard, and select your input.
  4. Choose Auto or MIDI for answering exercises. Check that pressed notes appear.

Bluetooth MIDI availability depends on your keyboard, operating system and driver. The desktop app cannot guarantee that every Bluetooth keyboard will appear. USB usually gives the most predictable connection; wired speakers or headphones avoid Bluetooth audio delay.

Practice offline

The interface, built-in lessons, ear training, progression tools, fonts and all four sounds are included. Online song-library updates, cloud operations, YouTube and remote song analysis still require internet access.

Your saved work

Desktop progress is separate from your browser's progress. It stays in the app's local profile between launches and updates. Progression import and export use the existing controls. Use Guided Mode's existing progress export where available for a backup. Uninstalling or clearing app data can affect saved work; keep your exports.

Install and update

Download the Windows installer, or the Mac disk image matching your processor: Apple silicon (arm64) or Intel (x64). On Mac, drag Faithful Keys into Applications before opening it. Use Help → Check for updates to find newer downloads, quit the app, and install the new version.

This free edition has no paid publisher certificate or Apple notarization. Your operating system may require confirmation before it opens. Only approve a download you obtained from the official release page and trust. If your computer's security policy blocks unsigned apps, follow that policy.
