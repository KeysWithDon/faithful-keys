How to read the mirror

Every note has a fixed place on the chromatic circle. The chord skeleton connects the root, third or suspension, and fifth. Extensions are shown separately. The home key stays highlighted in gold; a dotted arc shows the chord root’s relationship to it. Brief gold trails show estimated closest voice movements based on actual MIDI octaves. Sustain, velocity and all 88 physical note events remain tracked without a MIDI chart. Demo works without a keyboard.
